# Oxygen Data Sources

This scaffold does not make live weather requests yet.

Planned initial sources:

- Open-Meteo — general forecast/current model estimate.
- MET Norway — forecast fallback.
- NOAA/NWS — United States official alerts and later observations/radar.
- Environment and Climate Change Canada — Canadian official alerts.
- Open-Meteo/CAMS — air-quality path where appropriate.

Before any provider ships, document its current terms, attribution, rate/caching requirements, privacy implications, and last review date under `docs/data-sources/`.
